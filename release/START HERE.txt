FD Studio - fall alert for the wearer's laptop
==============================================

WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
  FD Studio.exe        the program
  alerts.bundle.json   phone-alert settings (sent to you privately - keep it
                       private, do not post it anywhere)
  START HERE.txt       this file

Keep all of them together in one folder, e.g. Documents\FD Studio\.


SETTING UP THE LAPTOP (once)
  1. Double-click FD Studio.exe.
     If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": More info -> Run anyway.
  2. Turn on Bluetooth, and switch on the pendant.
  3. In FD Studio: Scan -> choose "falldetect-datalog" -> Connect.
  4. The wearer puts the pendant on and stands still.
     Click "Calibrate upright" and hold still for 3 seconds.
  5. Leave FD Studio open, the laptop plugged in, and sleep turned off
     (Windows Settings -> System -> Power -> Sleep: Never).


FAMILY: GET ALERTS ON YOUR PHONE
  1. On the laptop: User tab -> "Get alerts on your phone".
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone camera. Telegram opens -> press START.
  3. For a LOUD alarm too: follow the ntfy steps in the bot's reply.
  To stop the alerts: send /stop to the bot.


WHAT HAPPENS ON AN ALERT
  Possible fall : the laptop beeps for 30 s. No response -> loud alarm, and
                  every phone gets a Telegram message and a loud ntfy alert.
  SOS           : hold the pendant button for 1.5 s (or "Hold for SOS" on the
                  laptop). Loud alarm and phone alerts straight away.
  Pendant button: a short tap during an alert means "I'm OK".
  Laptop        : "Silence alarm" stops the sound only.
                  "They're OK - dismiss" ends the alert and tells the phones.


LIMITS - PLEASE READ
  - Alerts go out only while this laptop is ON, ONLINE and FD Studio is OPEN.
  - Only ONE running FD Studio may use this Telegram bot at a time.
  - This is a prototype, not a medical alarm service. It calls nobody.
